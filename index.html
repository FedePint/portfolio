About Me

I am a Computer Engineering Master's Student at Politecnico di Torino, with a transversal background uniting high-performance parallel computing (C++, CUDA, low-level hardware architectures) and real-time 3D computer graphics (OpenGL, GLSL, Unreal Engine 5, Blender).

After completing my Bachelor's Degree in Cinema and Media Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, I pursued the Master's Degree in Computer Engineering at the same institution. This dual academic foundation empowers me to master both the mathematical and visual aspects of 3D rendering engines and lookdev pipelines, as well as software engineering, computer architectures, and hardware-accelerated computing on CPUs and GPUs.

Passionate about hardware optimization, memory bandwidth, and real-time graphics pipelines, I bridge the gap between engineering rigor and artistic vision — from writing coalesced GPU kernels and bare-metal microcontroller routines to sculpting photorealistic environments and authoring custom shader networks.
